Automation

Two things run without anyone pressing a button. Both stay inside each channel's pacing, so no channel is flooded.

Post the live listings Each channel with Autopilot on posts one home at a time from the live inventory: newest first, in the hours and the daily limit you set on the channel. A home goes out again only after its repeat period.
Share agencies' posts When an agency posts a listing, Synapse's channels that ask for it post it too, with a link that still credits the agency. Agencies are not asked first: this is Synapse sharing what they publish.

Team

Who can open the studio. Enter an email and Synapse sends an invitation: the link in it brings them to a page where they choose a password and create their account as part of the team. Someone who already has a Synapse account just gets access. An admin can invite and remove anyone.
