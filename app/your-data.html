Erase my personal data

Removes everything we hold about you: your conversations with Tayo, your income, occupation and household details, the places you told us matter to you, your saved homes, your dream board, and the record of which listings you opened. It cannot be undone.

Your sign-in stays. We keep your email address and password so the account still works — nothing else. If you want the account itself gone as well, say so when you write to us and we will remove it; you are entitled to that and we will not argue about it.

If an agency has already been introduced to you, they keep the record that an introduction happened — which home, which agency, and when. Your name, number, budget and preferences are removed from it.
