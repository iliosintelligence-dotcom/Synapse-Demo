Palette

Near-black and off-white unchanged. Indigo is the only addition — adire's own colour, carrying verification. Burnt orange stays the warm pip. Neither ever touches body text.

Illustration — “ink line”

One 2.1px stroke, round joins, a single accent fill and nothing else. No gradients, no second weight. Same hand as the feather icons already in the app, so the two never fight.

Adire motifs

Three motifs, redrawn as clean line. Used at 4–5% as a watermark behind heroes, empty states and verification — never as wallpaper, never behind data.

Texture

A fine grain sits over the whole page at 3.5% (it is on right now — tilt your screen). Plus one brush underline, used on a single emphasised phrase per page, never more.

Where this must not go: the Toju transcript, property cards, CRM tables and pricing. Those are read to make decisions, and texture behind them is noise. Personality lives in the connective tissue — heroes, empty states, section breaks, onboarding and verification.
