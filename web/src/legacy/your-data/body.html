Take a copy

Downloads everything listed above, from this device, as a JSON file. It is the data itself, not a summary. For a copy of what we hold on our servers, email ilios.intelligence@gmail.com and quote your identifier.

Erase my personal data

Removes everything we hold about you: your conversations with Tayo, your income, occupation and household details, the places you told us matter to you, your saved homes, your dream board, and the record of which listings you opened. It cannot be undone.

Your sign-in stays. We keep your email address and password so the account still works, nothing else. If you want the account itself gone as well, say so when you write to us and we will remove it; you are entitled to that and we will not argue about it.

If an agency has already been introduced to you, they keep the record that an introduction happened, which home, which agency, and when. Your name, number, budget and preferences are removed from it.

If you connected a social account

Agencies can connect a Facebook Page, an Instagram professional account, a TikTok account or a Telegram channel so Synapse can publish their listings there.

To remove Synapse's access: in the agency portal, open Social studio, then Channels, and press Disconnect on the account. Its access tokens are deleted at once. You can also remove Synapse from Facebook's Business integrations settings or TikTok's app permissions settings, or remove our bot from the Telegram channel.

To have everything deleted that we hold from a connected account, including the record that it was connected, email ilios.intelligence@gmail.com with the account's name. We confirm by email when it is done.

If you commented on an agency's post that Synapse published, we hold only your display name and the comment's text, and delete them automatically after 90 days. Email the same address with your name and the post to have them deleted sooner.
