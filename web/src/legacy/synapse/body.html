Add a channel

Go to Channels and press the orange Add a channel. A guide walks you through three steps:

  1. Pick the network. Facebook or Telegram: the networks Synapse connects to directly. Others will appear as Synapse connects to them.
  2. Facebook: press Continue with Facebook, sign in as an admin of Synapse's Page, tick the Page and leave every permission on. Facebook brings you back here: tick which Pages are Synapse's and add them.
  3. Telegram: write the channel's public name (like @SynapseLagos), then make the Synapse bot an admin of the channel with Post messages on. The channel switches itself on as soon as Telegram tells Synapse.
  4. Say what it is for. A new channel starts quiet. In Channels, switch on our own posts, autopilot or agency posts.

You can add as many accounts as you like, including several on the same network, one per city for example.

What each switch does

  • Switched on: off keeps the account on the page but sends it nothing.
  • Post homes by itself: the channel posts one live listing at a time, newest first, inside the hours and daily limit you set.
  • Share agencies' posts: when an agency publishes a listing, this channel posts it too, crediting the agency.
  • What this page takes: you decide which listings go where. Tick the kinds (for sale, for rent, land...), set bedrooms, a price range or areas, and the card shows how many live listings match. A listing goes to every page whose rules it matches, and a rule left empty takes anything.

Open Name, city and pacing on a card to rename it, give it a city, and set posts a day, hours between posts and the hours it may post (Lagos time).

Create a post

  1. Start from a real home (optional). Tap a listing: its photos are added and the caption is written from its real details.
  2. Say what it is about and press Write captions. Three captions appear in Synapse's voice. Tap one, then edit it freely.
  3. Add pictures of your own with the + box (JPG, PNG or WEBP, up to 8 MB each). For YouTube, add one vertical video instead (MP4 or MOV, up to 3 minutes and 50 MB) and tick only YouTube.
  4. Tap the accounts that should get it, then choose Now or Pick a time.
  5. Choose Now, Pick a time, or Several times. With several times you add as many dates as you like (or press Repeat daily or weekly to fill them in), and the same post goes out at each, on every account you ticked.
  6. Press Post (or Schedule). To line up a week of different posts, press Schedule and write another: it keeps your accounts and times and clears only the post. Scheduled posts show below by day, and Stop all in one batch cancels a whole batch.
  7. The preview on the right shows roughly how it will look.

Automation: two things run on their own

Post the live listings uses the channels with Autopilot on. Share agencies' posts is one master switch on the Automation page: when it is on, every channel set to "Share agencies' posts" also posts what agencies publish.

So there are two switches to think about: the master one in Automation, and the per-channel one in Channels. Both must be on for a channel to share agency posts.

Scheduled Waiting for its time. You can still press Stop.
Sending On its way to the network.
Sent, not live yet Accepted; the network has not confirmed it yet.
Live Confirmed on the network.
Failed It did not go out. The reason is shown under it.
Stopped You cancelled it before it went.

Team (admins only)

Enter their email, pick a role (Social media manager or Admin) and press Send invitation. The link brings them to a page where they enter their name and a password. If the email cannot be sent, the page gives you a link to copy and send yourself. You can remove anyone's access here at any time.

Facebook shared no Page

Add a channel again and, when Facebook lists Pages, tick Synapse's. If Facebook does not ask, remove Synapse under Facebook Settings, Business integrations, then try again.

My Telegram channel stays off

The Synapse bot must be an admin of the channel with Post messages on, and the name you wrote must be the channel's public @name.

A post says the Page needs connecting again

Facebook's permission ended. Add a channel, Facebook, and tick the same Page: its settings are kept.

A post says Failed

Read the reason under it. Often it is a missing picture on Instagram, or the account needs reconnecting. Fix that and create the post again.

Agencies' posts are not appearing on my channel

Check both switches: Share agencies' posts in Automation, and on the channel itself. And that the channel is switched on.

I cannot see the Team page

Only admins see it. Ask an admin to change your role.
