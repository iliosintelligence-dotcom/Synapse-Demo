## 0. Purpose & guardrail summary

This document defines how Synapse acquires **qualified Lagos buyers and renters** — people with a real, specific housing need Tayo can actually help — and builds the trust that is the product's core promise ("no ghost listings"). It deliberately leads with the **consumer/Tayo story**, not the Agency OS, per the flywheel sequencing (buyer trust first, agency value second).

**Success is not downloads or raw traffic.** Success is a person who arrives, tells Tayo something true about their life, and leaves with more clarity or a better-fit home than they had before. A large, unqualified audience is a failure mode here, not a win — it degrades the match quality and the trust signal.

**Load-bearing legal constraints (from the memo, do not violate):**
- No guarantee-style claims: not "zero surprises," not "your naira is safe," not "escrow-protected," not "guaranteed returns/savings," not "we guarantee you'll find/save/earn." (Memo §3D, §6.) These contradict Tayo's own no-guarantees doctrine.
- "Verified" only means the **7-check process** (title, structure, flood, legal, ownership, media, freshness) passed on a date and was re-confirmed within 14 days. It is a **process warranty, not an outcome guarantee** — never let a creative imply Synapse guarantees the deal, the condition, the price, or the title. (Memo §2B, §3.)
- No fabricated stats. No yield/return/price/savings figure in any creative unless the underlying number is real AND carries "estimate, not a guarantee — verify independently." (Memo §6.) For launch marketing the safest posture is to **avoid quantified outcome claims entirely** until the verification desk and real usage data can back them.
- Any public page rendering Restaglick/Bromolek needs the commercial font license first; otherwise Fraunces/Inter fallback. (Memo §4.) Affects launch creatives that reuse brand faces.
- The moment a real buyer link goes public, Gate 1 items must be live (privacy notice, AI disclosure, verified/unverified labels, verification-standards). Marketing must not send public traffic to a Tayo entry point that lacks these. (Memo §0, §7.) **This is a marketing dependency, not just a legal one** — flag to PM/legal before any campaign link goes out.

## 1. Positioning one-pager

### The one-line positioning
**Synapse is Nigeria's AI property advisor — you talk to Tayo about the life you're trying to build, and Tayo finds you real, verified homes that fit. Not a wall of listings. A conversation that ends in the right few.**

### The category problem we're reframing
Most Lagos house-hunters do not need to be taught what a property website is — they've used them, and they've been burned by them. The real problem is not *access to listings*; Lagos has a flood of listings across WhatsApp groups, Instagram/Facebook pages, and listing sites. The problem is **uncertainty and wasted effort**:
- Ghost listings and bait ads — the beautiful flat that "just got taken," the agent who wants an inspection fee for a property that doesn't exist.
- Endless scrolling and repetition — telling ten different agents the same requirements, in ten different chats.
- No trusted filter — no way to know which agent, agency, or listing is real before you've spent time, transport money, and hope.

Synapse's job in marketing is to move the buyer from "another listings app" to a genuinely different mental model: **you don't search, you talk — and what comes back is filtered for reality first.**

### What makes us different (the wedge)
1. **Tayo, an advisor — not a search box.** You describe your situation (budget, area, family, commute, what matters), and Tayo reasons about trade-offs and asks the one question that most changes your options. It educates before it sells.
2. **Verified-first.** Tayo recommends only listings that passed Synapse's 7-point verification and were re-confirmed within 14 days. The promise is "no ghost listings" — framed honestly as a process, not a guarantee.
3. **Qualification, not volume.** Because Tayo understands your need, you see a short, reasoned shortlist with *why* each fits — instead of 200 tabs.

### Who we're for (and who we're not chasing)
- **Primary:** Lagos renters and buyers with a **specific, near-term need** — professionals relocating within/into Lagos, families upsizing, people renewing/escaping a bad lease, diaspora buyers who can't physically inspect and need trust most of all.
- **Not the target for launch:** idle browsers, "just looking" traffic with no criteria, or reach-for-reach's-sake audiences. They inflate numbers and dilute the qualified signal.

### The vibe (brand tone)
Warm, premium, calm, honest. Terracotta/off-white, glassmorphism. Tayo sounds like a knowledgeable friend who happens to know the market cold — never hypey, never a hard-sell, never over-promising. **Our restraint is a feature:** in a market full of "100% guaranteed" agent captions, the brand that refuses to over-claim reads as the trustworthy one.

### The strategic sequencing (do not break)
Buyer trust is built **before** agency-side acquisition marketing. We do not run agency-recruitment campaigns ("list with us / get qualified leads") into the public market until the buyer experience is trustworthy and Gate 1 is live. If a channel opportunity tempts us to pull agency acquisition forward, check with the product-manager subagent on where the product actually is before acting.

## 2. Messaging pillars

Four pillars. Each has the idea, an outcome-led angle, on-tone example lines, and the claim ceiling. **Example lines are directional, not final copy** — social-media-manager writes the calendar; counsel clears anything public.

### Pillar 1 — Talk, don't scroll (Tayo as advisor)
- **Idea:** Replace the search-and-scroll ritual with a conversation that understands your life.
- **Outcome:** Find homes faster; stop repeating yourself to ten agents.
- **Directional lines:** "Stop scrolling. Start talking." / "Tell Tayo what your day looks like — it'll tell you where to live." / "One conversation instead of forty open tabs."
- **Claim ceiling:** Fine to say Tayo is an AI advisor that understands your needs and shortlists fewer, better-fit homes. Must not imply Tayo is human, a licensed agent/lawyer/financial adviser, or that its guidance is a guarantee (memo §2A). "Faster" is a process claim, safe; avoid a specific time promise.

### Pillar 2 — Real homes only (verified, no ghost listings)
- **Idea:** Every home Tayo recommends passed a documented verification process and was re-confirmed recently.
- **Outcome:** Avoid fake listings, bait ads, and inspection-fee scams.
- **Directional lines:** "No ghost listings." / "Seven checks before a home reaches you." / "If Tayo shows it to you, it's real and it's recent." / "Tired of 'it just got taken'? So were we."
- **Claim ceiling:** THE most legally sensitive pillar. "Verified" = the 7 checks passed on a date + 14-day re-confirm, and that's ALL it means. Never "zero surprises," never "your naira is safe," never imply guaranteed condition/title/price. Any creative using the badge must be reinforceable by "here's exactly what verified means" (memo §2B, §3C). "No ghost listings" is acceptable as the honest anti-fraud goal — pair it with the process framing, not with a safety guarantee.

### Pillar 3 — A shortlist, with the why (personalised, reasoned)
- **Idea:** You get a few strong matches with the reasoning and trade-offs, not an undifferentiated dump.
- **Outcome:** Receive personalised recommendations; decide with more clarity and less doubt.
- **Directional lines:** "Not 200 results. The right 3 — and why." / "Tayo shows its work." / "Homes that fit your budget, your commute, and your actual life."
- **Claim ceiling:** Present recommendations as informed guidance, not valuations or financial advice. If a price/commute/fit figure ever appears, it's an "estimate — verify independently," never a promise (memo §2A, §6). Prefer qualitative framing over numbers in launch creatives.

### Pillar 4 — Honest by design (trust as the product)
- **Idea:** Synapse tells you what it knows, what it doesn't, and when to get a professional — and that honesty is the point.
- **Outcome:** Reduce uncertainty; make a big decision with a straight-talking guide.
- **Directional lines:** "Tayo won't promise you the moon. It'll show you the numbers and what to check." / "We'd rather tell you the truth than make the sale." / "Verified where we can verify — and honest where we can't."
- **Claim ceiling:** This pillar is where restraint becomes the brand asset. It lets us turn the legal guardrails into a *marketing advantage*. Must genuinely reflect product behaviour (Tayo's doctrine already does this), so it's defensible. Do not overstate verification coverage — "verified where we can verify" is honest; "everything is verified/safe" is not.

**Cross-pillar tone rules:** educate before selling; outcomes over features; warm not hypey; never guarantee; when in doubt, under-claim. Flag any new claim about pricing, savings, guarantees, or agency vetting to legal before it's published.

## 3. Channel plan — meet Lagos buyers where they are, then convert to a Tayo conversation

**Strategic logic:** Lagos house-hunting already happens in WhatsApp groups, Instagram/Facebook property pages and groups, and on listing sites. We don't fight that gravity — we **insert Synapse into those existing surfaces as the trustworthy, less-exhausting alternative**, and route intent to a single high-intent action: **start a conversation with Tayo.** Pre-seed budget means bias to channels with fast feedback loops and a clear qualified-user signal over broad paid reach.

**The one conversion event that matters:** a visitor starts a Tayo chat AND supplies real criteria (budget + area + deal-type at minimum). Everything upstream is just getting the *right* people to that door. (This is also why the Tayo entry point must have Gate 1 disclosures live before any public link — §0.)

### Tier 1 — Primary (start here; strongest qualified signal, cheapest feedback loop)

**A. WhatsApp — the home turf.**
- *Why:* This is where Lagos property conversations already live; it's conversational, which matches Tayo's format natively.
- *Play:* Seed a small number of relevant existing property/relocation/estate WhatsApp communities with genuinely useful content (a short "how to spot a ghost listing" explainer, a "questions to ask before you pay an inspection fee" checklist), with a soft "or let Tayo do the filtering for you" CTA. Later, a WhatsApp Business presence as the front door to Tayo (note: WhatsApp Business API is an external dependency per the brief — flag to PM; don't assume it's available).
- *Qualified = * people who click through and start a Tayo chat with real criteria, not group members reached.

**B. Instagram — education + brand vibe.**
- *Why:* Where the Lagos property aesthetic and audience live; carousels/Reels are ideal for the "explain AI-powered discovery" job.
- *Play:* Educational carousels and short Reels built on the four pillars — lead with Pillar 1 (talk don't scroll) and Pillar 2 (no ghost listings). Warm/premium look. Comparison content: "the old way (40 tabs, 10 agents, 3 ghost listings) vs. one conversation." Organic first; only put spend behind posts that already earn saves/shares (fast feedback loop).
- *Qualified = * profile-link taps that become Tayo conversations; secondarily saves/shares of educational content (intent-to-return signal).

**C. Facebook groups — where the searching actually happens.**
- *Why:* Lagos "houses/flats for rent" Facebook groups are enormous and high-intent, full of people posting "looking for a 2-bed in Yaba under ₦X."
- *Play:* Value-first participation (not spam): answer real "looking for…" posts with genuinely helpful guidance and, where appropriate, "Tayo can shortlist verified options for that brief." Respect group rules — this is relationship, not blast. Ideal early qualified-lead source because the need is already stated.
- *Qualified = * a group member who states a brief, converts to a Tayo chat, and Tayo can actually serve them.

### Tier 2 — Secondary (scale once Tier 1 proves message-market fit)

**D. Listing-site adjacency / intercept.**
- *Why:* People on existing listing sites are mid-search and already frustrated by ghost listings — perfect context for our wedge.
- *Play:* Content and (later) targeted placements that meet the searcher's frustration: "Been burned by a listing that wasn't real? Try describing what you want instead." Position Synapse as the trust layer, not another listing wall. Keep it honest — no comparative claim we can't back.
- *Qualified = * intercepted searchers who convert to a reasoned Tayo conversation.

**E. Micro-influencers / trusted voices (relocation, diaspora, "moving to Lagos").**
- *Why:* Trust transfers through people. Diaspora buyers especially need a trusted intermediary because they can't inspect in person — our verification story is strongest exactly here.
- *Play:* A few credible relocation/real-estate/diaspora creators demoing a real Tayo conversation. Authentic walkthroughs > scripted ads. Start tiny, measure qualified conversions per creator, then concentrate spend.
- *Qualified = * referred users who start real Tayo conversations; watch conversion-per-creator, not follower counts.

### Tier 3 — Later / conditional
- **Search (Google) intent capture** ("verified apartments Lagos," "is [listing site] legit," "avoid rent scam Lagos") — high intent but higher cost; turn on once the funnel converts.
- **Diaspora-targeted** (UK/US Nigerians buying/renting remotely) — powerful for the verification pillar, but only after the buyer experience and verification desk are demonstrably solid, since these users are the least forgiving of a broken trust promise.

### What we explicitly deprioritise for launch
- Broad brand-awareness spend, billboards, mass paid reach — no qualified signal, slow feedback, wrong for pre-seed.
- Anything optimised for downloads/installs as the headline metric.
- **All agency-recruitment messaging in public channels** — sequencing rule; buyer trust first.

### Measurement — what "qualified" means, concretely
Primary metric across every channel: **qualified Tayo conversations** = a chat where the visitor supplies real criteria (budget + area + deal-type) and receives a reasoned shortlist. Supporting signals: return visits, dream-board creation, saved homes, share/save of educational content. **Vanity metrics we will not optimise for:** impressions, raw followers, downloads, group-members-reached. Every channel gets judged on qualified conversations per naira and speed of feedback, not reach.

## 4. Messaging guardrails — can / cannot (claim-check table)

| Theme | We CAN say (on-tone, defensible) | We CANNOT say (violates memo / doctrine) |
|---|---|---|
| Verification | "Seven checks before a home reaches you." "No ghost listings." "Verified and re-confirmed within 14 days." "Verified where we can verify — honest where we can't." | "Zero surprises." "Your naira is safe." "Escrow-protected." "Guaranteed real / guaranteed safe." Anything implying condition/title/price is guaranteed. |
| Tayo | "Nigeria's AI property advisor." "Tayo understands your needs and shortlists fewer, better-fit homes." "Guidance to help you decide." | "Tayo guarantees you'll find/save/earn." Implying Tayo is human, a licensed agent, lawyer, or financial adviser. |
| Outcomes | "Find homes faster." "Stop scrolling, start talking." "A shortlist with the why." "Less uncertainty." | Specific savings/return/time guarantees. "Save ₦X." "X% returns." "Find your home in Y days." |
| Numbers/stats | Only real, sourced figures, each tagged "estimate — verify independently." Prefer qualitative framing at launch. | Any fabricated or unverifiable stat. Yields/prices as promises. Made-up user counts or success rates. |
| Agencies (not a launch theme) | Nothing public yet — sequencing rule. | "Verified agencies" as a marketing claim before the agency-KYC process actually backs it (memo §5.1). |
| Fonts (public creatives) | Use licensed faces only once the commercial license is secured; else Fraunces/Inter fallback. | Ship public creatives in Restaglick/Bromolek under demo/personal-use license (memo §4). |

**Escalation rule:** any new claim touching pricing, savings, guarantees, returns, or agency vetting goes to the legal/compliance agent before publishing. When uncertain, under-claim — restraint is on-brand and it's our trust moat.

**Dependency flag for PM/legal:** no public campaign link should point at a Tayo entry point until Gate 1 disclosures (privacy notice, AI disclosure, verified/unverified labels, verification-standards) are live in the UI. Marketing launch timing is gated on this.
