## 0. Claim-safety checklist — run this on EVERY post before it schedules

Do not schedule a post until all seven are true. If any is uncertain, route to the legal/compliance agent (escalation rule, memo §6). When in doubt, under-claim — restraint is the brand asset.

1. **No guarantee language.** None of: "zero surprises," "your naira is safe," "escrow-protected," "guaranteed," "risk-free," "we guarantee you'll find/save/earn," "100%." (memo §3D, §6; GTM §0.)
2. **"Verified" scoped correctly.** Used only to mean the **7-check process** (title, structure, flood, legal, ownership, media, freshness) passed on a date and was re-confirmed within 14 days — a **process warranty, not an outcome guarantee.** Never imply Synapse guarantees the deal, condition, title, or price. Any post using the word must be reinforceable by "here's exactly what verified means." (memo §2B, §3.)
3. **No fabricated stats.** No user counts, success rates, yields, prices, savings, or "% of scams avoided" unless the number is real AND carries "estimate — verify independently." Launch default: **avoid quantified outcome claims entirely.** (memo §6; GTM §4.)
4. **No implying features that aren't live.** Do not depict proximity/geo-alerts, agency-side tooling, escrow/payments, or Toju-negotiator to buyers as available now. Toju chat + verified-listing matching is what exists — market that. (brief; GTM §5.)
5. **Toju described honestly.** "Nigeria's AI property advisor" / "AI advisor" — never implied to be a human, licensed agent, lawyer, or financial adviser; its guidance is help-to-decide, not a promise. (memo §2A; GTM pillar 1.)
6. **Brand-compliant visuals.** Warm off-white `#f7efe7`, terracotta (`--brand #FF7B2C` decorative only; text uses `--brand-deep #C2552B` / `--brand-text #A64420`), EB Garamond (display/headings/prices) + Lato (body/UI/caps eyebrows). No other fonts, no off-brand Canva template colours. (DESIGN_SYSTEM §1.) Terracotta is never body-text or under white text at small sizes (a11y F1/F2).
7. **Gate 1 checked before the CTA link is live.** See dependency below.

## 2. Repeatable content series (the engine)

Five series carry the whole calendar so nothing is invented from scratch weekly. Each maps to a pillar and a claim ceiling.

1. **"Ghost Listing of the Week"** (Pillar 2 — verified/no ghost listings). A composite/anonymised bait-ad pattern (never a real named agent/agency/listing — that invites defamation risk); we point at the red flags and show *how we'd verify it* against the 7 checks. **[LEGAL]** on any verification-mechanics wording. Never claims we catch every scam.
2. **"Meet Toju"** (Pillar 1 — talk, don't scroll). Watch Toju narrow a search in ~3 questions, or handle a hard question honestly (including saying "I can't promise that — here's what to check"). Screen-recorded from real product behaviour; voice must match Toju's doctrine (educate before persuade, no guarantees). Coordinate any transcript with the toju-ai agent so the voice is identical, not a lookalike.
3. **"House-hunt School"** (Pillars 1 & 3 — education). Plain-language explainers for Lagos renters/buyers: agency fees, C of O vs governor's consent, "agreement/agency/legal/caution" fee breakdown, what to inspect, questions to ask before paying an inspection fee. Pure value; earns the trust before any pitch.
4. **"Real Home, Real Checks"** (Pillar 2/3 — verified spotlight, reasoned shortlist). A verified home with the honest framing of what "verified" did and didn't check. No price promises; any figure is "estimate — verify independently." **[LEGAL]** on badge wording.
5. **"Neighbourhood in 60 seconds"** (Pillar 3 — reasoned, local knowledge). Calm, useful area guides (commute reality, day-vs-night feel, who it suits) — qualitative, not quantified. Demonstrates the market knowledge behind Toju without over-claiming.

Rotating sixth slot (Sat / behind-the-scenes): **"Honest by design"** (Pillar 4) — short posts on why we refuse to over-promise, what Toju *won't* say, how verification actually works behind the curtain. This is where restraint becomes the marketing.

## 4. Community-engagement playbook (Facebook + WhatsApp groups)

This is our strongest qualified-lead surface (GTM §3C) — and the easiest place to get banned or read as spam. Rule: **be the most helpful person in the thread, whether or not it converts.** Relationship, not blast.

### Principles
- **Value first, link last (or never).** Answer the actual question fully *before* any mention of Synapse. If a great answer needs no mention of us, give it anyway — that's the reputation compounding.
- **Respect group rules absolutely.** Many Lagos housing groups ban promotion. Read pinned rules; where promo is banned, contribute value only and let people find us via profile. Getting the account banned costs more than any single lead.
- **Disclose honestly.** When you do mention Synapse, be transparent that you're from the team ("full disclosure, I work on Synapse"). Hidden shilling destroys the trust we're selling.
- **One mention, no repetition.** Never post the same reply across threads/groups (spam-flag magnet). Tailor every response to the specific brief.
- **Claim ceilings apply in comments too.** No "guaranteed," no invented stats, no "verified" beyond the 7-check meaning, no depicting unbuilt features. A comment is public copy.
- **Never argue or trash competitors.** If someone had a bad experience elsewhere, empathise and be useful — don't dunk. Restraint reads as trustworthy.

### Response templates (adapt every time — never paste verbatim)

**A. Someone posts a specific brief ("Looking for a 2-bed in Yaba under ₦X, moving September"):**
> "Yaba's a solid pick for mainland access on that budget — a couple of honest things to weigh: [genuine area/trade-off insight]. Before you pay any inspection fee, confirm the exact unit is real and still available this week. Full disclosure, I work on Synapse — you can describe that exact brief to Toju, our AI advisor, and it'll shortlist verified options with the reasoning. Either way, happy to point you in the right direction."

**B. Someone's been burned / venting about a scam:**
> "That's exactly the 'it just got taken after I paid to view' pattern — really sorry, it's far too common. For next time: [2–3 concrete red-flag checks]. This is the whole reason we run seven checks before a listing reaches people on Synapse, but honestly, run those checks on anything from anywhere."

**C. Someone asks a general education question (fees, C of O, what to inspect):**
> Give the complete, genuinely useful answer with **no** pitch. Optionally close with "we put a fuller breakdown on our page if it helps" — only if the group permits, and only once.

**D. Where promotion is banned:** answer with pure value, no mention, no link. Let the username and consistent helpfulness do the work.
